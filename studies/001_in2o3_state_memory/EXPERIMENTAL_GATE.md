# Experimental gate

## Gate A — delayed-reactivation kill test

Reproduce the S1→S3→CO2-only-reoxidation sequence in independent catalyst beds from one synthesis batch. After reoxidation, return to the unchanged standard reaction feed and continue until kinetic stabilization rather than stopping at the published 90-min comparison point.

Primary paired quantity: stabilized post-regeneration methanol STY divided by the bed's own S1 baseline.

- If stabilized recovery reaches at least 95% of S1 without reversal of the measured morphology proxy, stop the persistent-memory headline and study slow reactivation kinetics.
- If a reproducible deficit remains, advance to Gate B.

## Gate B — endpoint-matched regeneration

Compare at least two regeneration paths that reach the same operando In redox endpoint, beginning with dry CO2-only regeneration at the published temperature versus the lowest feasible lower temperature. Use identical post-regeneration standard-feed exposure.

Primary causal test: do endpoint-matched paths yield different independent accessible area or area-normalized methanol rate?

Add trace-H2O arms only if the dry temperature contrast is feasible and hydroxyl-mediated surface chemistry remains unresolved.

## Quality requirements

- paired within-batch comparisons;
- full GC/MS time series and carbon balance;
- spectrum-level XANES endpoint matching;
- whole-pattern/multi-peak XRD with instrumental broadening control;
- independent accessible-area/site-count proxy for a morphology claim;
- surface/phase-sensitive control before claiming intrinsic chemistry;
- predeclared exclusions and precision target.
