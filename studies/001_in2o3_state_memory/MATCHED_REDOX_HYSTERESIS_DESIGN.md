# Matched-redox hysteresis design

## Question

At identical temperature, pressure, instantaneous feed composition, and matched ensemble-average In redox spectrum, can Zr-doped In2O3 retain different methanol rates depending on prior feed history?

A positive result would show that the mean redox coordinate is not a sufficient catalyst-state descriptor and would motivate a slower hidden coordinate such as vacancy topology, surface/subsurface defect allocation, hydroxyl organization, or reversible local Zr–O–In restructuring.

## Reversible composition loop

Stay away from the severe 0.08 vol-% CO2 over-reduction regime. Use a down/up loop such as:

`20 -> 10 -> 5 -> 2 -> 1.7 -> 2 -> 5 -> 10 -> 20 vol-% CO2`

Hold H2 fraction, total flow, temperature, pressure, catalyst mass, and analysis protocol fixed; replace CO2 with N2.

## Mandatory artifact control

Before interpreting a kinetic loop, measure an inert-tracer step under matched reactor conditions and estimate the reactor+analyzer transfer function. Correct gas/product transients for dead volume and analyzer delay.

## State matching

Do not call two states redox-equivalent merely because a fitted average valence rounds to the same number. Require spectrum-level In K-edge equivalence within a frozen repeatability envelope and no evidence of metallic In or irreversible structural drift.

## Frozen promotion rule

Promote a hidden-state claim only if, after transfer-function correction and spectrum-level redox matching, at least two internal composition points show reproducible path-dependent methanol-rate differences of at least 8%, with at least one point reaching 10%, and the final 20% CO2 state closes back to the initial structural/kinetic baseline.

## Falsifiers

- matched path differences remain below 5%;
- direction changes across independent cycles;
- differences disappear after full-spectrum redox matching;
- metallic In or persistent structural drift appears;
- final 20% CO2 baseline does not close;
- inert-tracer convolution reproduces the apparent loop.

## DFT trigger

Only after a matched-redox hysteresis signal survives these controls should DFT compare equal-vacancy-count states with different vacancy topology, hydroxylation, or surface/subsurface allocation. No broad ML potential is justified before the hidden coordinate is experimentally licensed.
