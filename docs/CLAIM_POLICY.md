# Claim policy

Every research claim should be tagged conceptually as one of:

1. **Observed** — directly reported or measured.
2. **Derived** — computed from observed quantities with an explicit transformation.
3. **Interpreted** — mechanistic explanation consistent with evidence but not uniquely identified.
4. **Hypothesized** — prospective claim awaiting adjudication.
5. **Falsified/stopped** — a branch that failed a frozen decision rule.

Do not promote an interpretation to an observation. Do not replace missing replicate uncertainty with decimal-rounding intervals. Do not use DFT to explain a small unresolved rate residual before experiment identifies the state variable and active-area contribution.
