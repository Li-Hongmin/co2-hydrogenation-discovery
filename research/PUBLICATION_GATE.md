# Publication gate

A conventional catalysis manuscript requires more than a reanalysis of published point estimates.

Minimum independent result:

- reproducible raw-data or new-experiment evidence that separates delayed reactivation from a persistent working-state difference;
- an independent state/structure measurement associated with the kinetic change;
- a prospective intervention or matched-state comparison that discriminates mechanisms;
- uncertainty and artifact controls appropriate to the claimed effect.

A computational paper-level claim additionally requires a physically licensed model and a mechanism-specific energetic contrast that survives matched-state controls; training an ML potential is not itself the scientific contribution.
