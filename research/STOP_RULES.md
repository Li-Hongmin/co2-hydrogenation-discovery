- Stop persistent morphology-memory framing if S4 activity returns to >=95% of S1 after kinetic stabilization without morphology reversal.
- Stop treating single-peak XRD width as an area proxy if corrected multi-peak/independent area measurements do not support it.
- Stop localized-Zr direct-surface mechanism if the motif is structurally unstable or all matched Stage-1 shifts are <0.05 eV.
- Stop broad DFT/MLIP expansion if no experimentally licensed hidden coordinate survives.
